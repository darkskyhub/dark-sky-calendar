Autumn

The Southern Cross climbs in the south-east. Carina and Omega Centauri hold the early evening as the Milky Way returns later.

Three months. Open a month for Full Moon, New Moon, Dark Nights, early-night planets and a short object list.

Dark Night

A Dark Night here means the early evening is actually dark: from 3 days after Full Moon through to 3 days after New Moon. The three days either side of Full Moon are poor for the Milky Way and faint objects.
